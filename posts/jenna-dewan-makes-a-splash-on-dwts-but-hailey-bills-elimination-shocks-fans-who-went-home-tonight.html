Jenna Dewan Makes a Splash on DWTS, but Hailey Bills’ Elimination Shocks Fans: Who Went Home Tonight?

Published on 2026-09-18 • By Vanrajsinh Vaghela
Dancing with the Stars Season 35 is already trending after Jenna Dewan’s 19/30 Tango and the elimination of Sarah Jane Nader and Hailey Bills. Here’s who went home, the latest DWTS cast details, scores and voting information.

Dancing with the Stars Season 35 is already generating major buzz, with Jenna Dewan, Hailey Bills, Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and several other DWTS names dominating online searches. Google Trends is currently showing rising interest in searches including “Jenna Dewan dancing with the stars,” “who got eliminated from DWTS tonight,” “DWTS cast,” “Hailey Bills DWTS,” “DWTS voting” and “who went home on DWTS tonight.”

So, who went home on DWTS tonight?

The Season 35 premiere was split across two nights, with one celebrity-pro couple eliminated at the end of each night. On Night 1, Conner Leavitt and professional partner Adele Zaikman were eliminated. On Night 2, Sarah Jane Nader and professional partner Hailey Bills were sent home. That leaves 14 celebrity couples still competing in the season. 1

Hailey Bills’ elimination is especially notable because Season 35 marked her promotion to the main professional cast. Bills had previously been part of the DWTS franchise and stepped into the professional lineup this season. She partnered with Sarah Jane Nader, creating the show's second all-female celebrity-pro pairing. 2

Sarah Jane Nader and Hailey Bills performed a Jive to Avril Lavigne’s “Sk8er Boi” during Night 2 of the premiere. The pair received 14 out of 30 from the judges before being eliminated following the combined scoring and viewer voting process. 3

Sarah Jane Nader also entered the competition with a familiar DWTS connection. She is the sister of Brooks Nader, who previously competed on Dancing with the Stars. That connection helped make searches for “Dancing with the Stars cast Brooks Nader” part of the wider Season 35 conversation.

Meanwhile, Jenna Dewan has become one of the biggest names connected to the new season.

Jenna Dewan and Val Chmerkovskiy performed a Tango to Blondie’s “Call Me” during Night 2. Their routine earned 19 out of 30 from judges Carrie Ann Inaba, Derek Hough and Bruno Tonioli. Inaba and Hough each gave the couple a 6, while Tonioli awarded them a 7. 4

Jenna’s appearance has attracted additional attention because of her long connection to dance. Her role in the 2006 movie Step Up helped establish her as a recognizable dancer and actress, and her appearance on DWTS brings her back into a competition ballroom environment.

But will Jenna Dewan’s dance background translate into a strong run on Dancing with the Stars?

That remains to be seen because DWTS requires celebrities to adapt to ballroom and Latin styles, rather than simply rely on previous dance experience.

Jenna and Val finished Night 2 with 19 points, while Maura Higgins and Mark Ballas topped the night’s leaderboard with 21 points. Harry Shum Jr. and Jenna Johnson also scored 21 points during the premiere week. 5

The Season 35 DWTS cast includes a wide range of celebrities, including Jenna Dewan, Harry Shum Jr., Julia Stiles, Amber Glenn, Ciara Miller, Maura Higgins, Giada De Laurentiis, Tatyana Ali, Tyler Cameron and others. The season began with 16 celebrity contestants. 6

Amber Glenn also attracted attention during Night 2. The Olympic figure skater performed a Cha Cha with Pasha Pashkov and received 18 out of 30. Julia Stiles and Ezra Sosa also scored 18, while Ciara Miller and Brandon Armstrong earned 15 points.

That explains why searches for “Amber DWTS” and “Ciara Dancing with the Stars” are also appearing alongside the broader Season 35 search trend.

Fans are also searching for “Bruno Dancing with the Stars” and “Bruno DWTS” because Bruno Tonioli remains one of the show's three judges alongside Carrie Ann Inaba and Derek Hough. Tonioli gave Jenna Dewan and Val a 7 for their Tango, the highest individual score the couple received that night.

Another major search topic is DWTS voting.

During live episodes, eligible viewers can vote for their favorite couples through the show's official voting system and text voting. Voting is tied to the live broadcast window, so viewers need to follow the instructions provided during the episode. The Season 35 premiere also experienced technical problems with online voting during Night 1, leading the show to direct viewers toward text voting. 7

If you are searching for the DWTS voting number or DWTS text number, make sure you use the voting instructions shown during the current live episode because voting details and contestant-specific instructions can change.

The biggest question right now is simple: who got eliminated from DWTS?

After the first two premiere nights, two couples have already left Season 35. Conner Leavitt and Adele Zaikman were eliminated first, followed by Sarah Jane Nader and Hailey Bills. 8

The next episode will bring the remaining couples together as the competition moves into its regular weekly format. Dancing with the Stars returns Tuesday, September 22, with the remaining celebrities competing together after the split premiere format. 9

For now, Jenna Dewan remains one of the biggest trending names from the new season, while searches for Hailey Bills, Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli, Amber Glenn, Ciara Miller, Brooks Nader and the full DWTS cast continue to drive interest around Season 35.

The first week has already delivered two eliminations, a new professional debut and several standout performances. The next live episode will reveal which remaining couples can build on their opening scores and which names become the next major DWTS elimination story.
