Kahani & Highlights

19 saal pehle Aaliyah ko khone ke baad former gangster Shivam Pandit (Emraan Hashmi) ek akeli zindagi jee raha hota hai. Ek din use ek anath bachi milti hai jiska naam wo Aaliyah rakhta hai, par child trafficking racket us bachi ko gayab kar deta hai. Bachi ko bachane ke liye Shivam Bangkok ke mafia Zorawar ke gang mein dakhil hota hai. Wahan uski mulaqat Zara (Disha Patani) se hoti hai, jo apne parivar ke jurm ke khilaf hai. Dono milkar trafficking ring se bachi aur baki bacchon ko bachane ke liye mission par nikalte hain
